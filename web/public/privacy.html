Balance Window Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026 · Native iOS app and open-source web app

In short: Balance Window works locally without an account. Cloud sync is optional. We do not sell personal data, run ads in the app, or track you across apps and websites.

Data you enter

Balance Window stores the accounts, balances, categories, one-time entries, recurring entries, notes, and preferences that you choose to enter. The native iOS app stores this data on your device by default. You can import and export a JSON backup at any time.

Optional Apple sign-in and cloud sync

You can use the core app without signing in. If you choose Sign in with Apple and enable cloud sync, we process an account identifier, the name and email address Apple may provide, session information, and an encrypted snapshot of your Balance Window data. Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your personal address.

These items are associated with your account and are used only to authenticate you, operate cloud backup and synchronization, resolve sync conflicts, and support account deletion. They are not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

What we do not collect in the iOS app

  • No advertising identifier, advertising data, or cross-app tracking.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, health, or fitness data.
  • No third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.
  • No bank connection, payment-card processing, trading, or credit scoring.
  • No transfer of your financial data to an AI service in version 1.0.

Retention, export, and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app, subject to your device backups. Exported JSON files are controlled by you. When signed in, you can delete the cloud copy or delete your account from Settings. Account deletion removes the account, active sessions, and cloud vault; the app also clears its local data after the deletion succeeds.

Service providers

Sign in with Apple provides authentication. Cloudflare Pages, Workers, and D1 host the website, API, account records, and encrypted cloud vault. GitHub hosts the public web source and issue tracker. These services participate only when the relevant feature or website is used. The native iOS app does not offer GitHub login.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, open an issue in the Balance Window issue tracker. Do not include financial data, Apple credentials, session cookies, tokens, or private keys in a public issue.

简体中文说明

核心说明

Balance Window 无需登录即可在本机使用。只有当你主动使用 Apple 登录和云同步时，服务才会处理账号标识、Apple 可能返回的姓名和邮箱、会话信息以及加密的 Balance Window 数据快照。这些数据仅用于登录、云备份、同步冲突处理和账号删除，不用于广告、营销或跨 App 跟踪。

iOS 1.0 不接入广告、第三方分析或崩溃上报 SDK，不收集定位、通讯录、照片、麦克风、健康或健身数据，也不会把财务数据发送给 AI 服务。你可以随时导入、导出、删除本机数据；登录后还可以删除云端副本或注销账号。
