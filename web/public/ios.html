QUESTIONS

A clear starting point.

Is Balance Window a budgeting or expense-tracking app?

Not in the traditional sense. Balance Window focuses on future balance and cash-flow timing. You can record important planned income and expenses without tracking every purchase.

Do I need to connect a bank?

No. Balance Window is designed for manual, local-first planning and does not require a bank connection.

Do I need an account?

No. The core app works locally without signing in. Sign in with Apple is optional and enables cloud backup and sync.

What should I add to the forecast?

Start with current balances, recurring paychecks, rent or mortgage, insurance, card payments, utilities, subscriptions, and any larger expense you already plan to make.

Is the forecast a real-time bank balance?

No. It is a planning projection based on the balances and money changes you enter. Actual results may differ.

How many accounts does version 1.0 support?

Version 1.0 supports up to two money accounts. Balance Window is a planning tool, not a bank, payment service, accounting system, or source of financial or investment advice.
